Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services and processes.

What documents do I need to purchase a car?

You'll need a valid driver's license, proof of insurance, and proof of income. If financing, we may also require additional documentation such as recent pay stubs or bank statements.

Do you accept trade-ins?

Yes! We accept trade-ins and offer competitive evaluations. Our team will assess your vehicle's condition and provide you with a fair market value estimate.

What financing options do you offer?

We offer various financing options including traditional auto loans, lease agreements, and special financing programs. Our finance team works with multiple lenders to find the best rates for your situation.

How long does the car buying process take?

The process typically takes 1-3 hours, depending on financing approval and paperwork. For cash purchases, it can be completed in as little as 30 minutes. We work efficiently to get you on the road quickly.
